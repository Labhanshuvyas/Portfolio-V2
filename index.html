Beyond technology

“When the obvious plan disappears, change the plan — not the goal.”

As a national-level netball player and school sports captain, I learned that leadership is often less about having the strongest individual player and more about understanding the team you actually have.

When several stronger players moved into a higher age group, I redistributed responsibilities around the younger players’ strengths and focused on confidence as much as execution.

That experience shapes how I approach product work: understand the constraints, work with the people available and adapt the plan without losing sight of the goal.
